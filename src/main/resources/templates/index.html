CoopPilot

An AI agent that tailors one-page LaTeX resumes and cover letters to co-op postings using only your real experience, and tracks your applications. LLM provider: mock

1 · Vault

Import your LaTeX resume and add experiences (F01).

2 · Postings

Paste a posting, analyze it, see your fit and keyword gaps (F02–F04).

3 · Tailor

Let the agent build a one-page resume, asking you when a fact is missing (F05–F07).

4 · Resumes

Revise in plain English with undo, write the cover letter, download .tex (F08–F10).

5 · Track

Log applications and move them through their statuses (F11).

6 · Dashboard

Progress against your daily and weekly goals (F12).
